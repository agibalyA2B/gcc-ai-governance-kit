# ADR 017: EU material as a note and an optional question, not a crosswalk column

**Status:** accepted, 2026-10-15

**Context.** The EU cited its first harmonised standard for high-risk AI, EN 18286 (quality management for providers),
on 14 October 2026. GCC vendors with EU clients will ask about it. The kit is GCC-first, the standard is paywalled, and
its article-coverage annex could not be checked.

**Decision.**
- A two-page sector note, beside the arbitration note, that starts with provider versus deployer, explains when the AI
  Act reaches a GCC organisation, what the presumption of conformity does and does not cover, why ISO/IEC 42001 is no
  substitute, and the Omnibus dates. Primary sources only; no article coverage is claimed.
- One optional question on the Details step, "Could this AI reach the EU?". Yes or Not sure shows a link to the note on
  the Details and tier screens and a line in the brief. The answer is stored outside the scored answers, so it cannot
  change the tier, the autonomy, the action matrix or the controls; a unit test holds that rule.

**Alternatives.** An EN 18286 crosswalk column: rejected (paywalled text, unverified coverage).

**Consequences.** The kit still establishes neither compliance nor certification, and says so.
