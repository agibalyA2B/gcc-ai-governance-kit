# GCC AI Governance Kit

> Adopt AI governance in one afternoon, in Arabic and English.

🚧 **Work in progress: v0.1 is being built in the open.**

An open, bilingual (AR/EN) starter kit for GCC organisations:
- register AI use cases;
- get an explainable risk tier, including agentic-AI factors;
- export the controls that apply under the UAE AI Charter, Dubai AI Ethics, SDAIA, ISO/IEC 42001 (clause references
  only), NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act.

Runs entirely in your browser. No login, no backend.

Live preview: https://agibalyA2B.github.io/gcc-ai-governance-kit/
